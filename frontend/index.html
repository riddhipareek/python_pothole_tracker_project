Why FixMyRoad Matters

Bridging the gap between silent road hazards and municipal governance.

Zero-Friction Reporting

Citizens can report road hazards instantly without registering or logging in. Automatic GPS geolocation records coordinates in one click.

90-Day SLA Enforcement

Unresolved potholes that exceed 90 days are automatically flagged on public maps and dashboards, preventing bureaucratic delays from going unnoticed.

Nearby Duplicate Alerts

Distance-based checks warn citizens when a pothole within 50 metres has already been reported, keeping the map clean for municipal engineering teams.
